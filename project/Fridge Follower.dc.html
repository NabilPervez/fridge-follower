Fridge Follower

Built from the PRD's jewel-tone tokens, Space Grotesk + Inter, 16px cards and 999px chips. Everything runs on local state, so the demo behaves like the offline app.

Try Fridge: tap a row to lower it, hold for the level sheet, swipe right for Full or left for Out. Shop: check an item to restock it to Full (undo lasts 5s). Tap a Plan badge to see which recipes need it. Today and Plan: tap a slot to pick a recipe or free text. Tap ✓ to mark it cooked, which lowers ingredient levels. Tweaks: 4 or 5 tabs (open question in the PRD), and fridge row density.
Assumptions Named "Fridge Follower" per the PRD's Name field. Dark theme only for now. Today is Wed, Sep 30; the week starts on Monday.
